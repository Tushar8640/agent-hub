Use it away from home

Install Tailscale on this PC and on your phone and sign in to the same account. The phone can then reach the hub from anywhere, without opening ports on your router. For an installable app with HTTPS, run tailscale serve for port 8650 and put the HTTPS address in publicUrl in config.yaml.
